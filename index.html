Safety First

Diwali Fireworks Safety Guidelines

Ensure a safe, joyful and memorable celebration with your family

✓

SAFETY DO'S

  • Always light fireworks outdoors in wide, open spaces away from buildings and dry vegetation.
  • Keep a bucket of clean water and sand readily available nearby for emergency damping.
  • Use an agarbatti (incense stick) or sparkler to light crackers from arm's length.
  • Wear well-fitted cotton clothes; ensure children celebrate under responsible adult guidance.
✕

SAFETY DON'TS

  • Never light crackers indoors, inside narrow balconies, or near parked vehicles.
  • Never put crackers inside glass bottles, metal tins, or containers to burst them.
  • Never bend over a firework while lighting or attempt to relight a dud or unexploded cracker.
  • Never carry loose crackers in pant pockets or store them near heat or cooking gas cylinders.
