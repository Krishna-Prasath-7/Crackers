Diwali Safety Guidelines

  • Always light fireworks outdoors in wide open spaces away from buildings.
  • Keep water buckets and sand nearby for emergency damping.
  • Use agarbatti (incense stick) to ignite fireworks at arm's length.
  • Ensure children celebrate strictly under adult supervision.
